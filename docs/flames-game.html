What is the Flames Game?

The flames game is a classic name-based relationship calculator that originated in schools during the 1990s. The word FLAMES stands for:

  • F — Friends
  • L — Love
  • A — Affection
  • M — Marriage
  • E — Enemies
  • S — Siblings
