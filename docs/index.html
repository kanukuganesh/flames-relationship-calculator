How Does the FLAMES Algorithm Work?

The FLAMES calculator follows a simple but magical process:

  1. Enter two names into the calculator
  2. Common letters between both names are cancelled out
  3. The remaining letters are counted
  4. That count is used to eliminate letters from F-L-A-M-E-S in a circular pattern
  5. The last letter standing reveals your relationship type!
